Hey {{contact.first_name}},

Quick follow-up on the free LLC setup and funding blueprints.

One of the most common reasons I see people get denied for business funding isn't their income. It's that they apply **in the wrong order.**

They go straight to lenders before:

- their business is set up correctly (LLC, EIN, business bank account, consistent business info everywhere), and
- their personal credit is cleaned up and ready to be reviewed.

The lender sees "new and risky" and says no. Every denial can add another hard inquiry to their credit, which makes the next application harder.

Here's the order the blueprints walk you through:

**Step 1: Set up the foundation.** Form your LLC and get the business basics in place. (We'll set up the LLC for free.)

**Step 2: Get your credit ready.** Review your reports, dispute anything inaccurate, and lower your utilization.

**Step 3: Apply strategically.** Start with the right lenders, in the right order, at the right time.

On a free call, we'll tell you which step you're on and exactly what to do next.
